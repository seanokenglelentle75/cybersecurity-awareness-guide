# Cybersecurity Awareness Guide
## 1. Passwords
Use a long password with letters, numbers and symbols.
Do not use the same password for email and banking.
Turn on 2FA  everywhere u can.

## 2. Phishing
Do not click links in unexpected email or SMS.
Check the sender email carefully. 
If a message says "urgent, verify now" , stop and check with the real company.
## 3. Public wifi
Do not log into banking on free public Wi-Fi.
Use mobile data or a VPN if you must.
Turn off auto-connect Wi-Fi
## 4. Social media
Do not share your location, ID numbers or password online.
Check your privacy settings
Do not accept friend requests from strangers
## 6. What To Do If Something Goes Wrong
1. Change your password immediately
2. Turn 2FA
3. Tell your IT team, lecture or bank.
4. Fill in the incident report template in this repo

Stay alert. Think before you click.
