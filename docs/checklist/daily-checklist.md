# Daily Cybersecurity Checklist
Check these everyday before you start work:
## Morning
- [ ] Phone and laptop are locked with PIN or fingerprint
- [ ] Antivirus is updated
- [ ] Check emails carefully- do not click strange links
## During the day
- [ ] Do not share password with anyone
- [ ] Do not use public wifi fo banking
- [ ] Lock your screen when you leave your desk
## End of the day
- [ ] Log out of all accounts
- [ ] Back up important files
- [ ] Report anything suspicious to IT
